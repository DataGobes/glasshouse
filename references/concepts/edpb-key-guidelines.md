# Concept: EDPB Guidelines Quick Reference

The European Data Protection Board issues binding (Art. 65) decisions and persuasive guidelines. Here are the ones most cited in scanner findings.

## On Consent
- **Guidelines 05/2020 on consent** — the core consent doctrine: freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous; cookie walls; bundled consent; granularity per purpose
- **Guidelines 03/2022 on dark patterns** in social media platform interfaces (joint with EDPS) — formal taxonomy: overloading, skipping, stirring, hindering, fickle, left in the dark
- **Binding Decision 01/2020** — Facebook & Pixel = joint controllers with website operators (cited in processor-transparency.md)

## On Tracking Technologies
- **Guidelines 2/2023 on the technical scope of Art. 5(3) ePrivacy** — landmark: confirmed fingerprinting, pixel tracking, and IP-based tracking all fall under Art. 5(3). Strictly-necessary exemption is narrowly construed.

## On Cross-Border Transfers
- **Recommendations 01/2020 on supplementary measures** — post-Schrems II, what to do beyond SCCs alone (encryption with EU-held keys, pseudonymisation, data localisation). EU-US DPF (2023) sits on top of this regime.
- **Recommendations 02/2020 on European essential guarantees** for surveillance — the test for whether a third country provides essentially-equivalent protection

## On Data Subject Rights
- **Guidelines 01/2022 on the right of access** (Art. 15) — proportionate identity verification; what must be in the response; format requirements
- **Guidelines 02/2024 on consent withdrawal** (in consultation) — clarifies Art. 7(3) "as easy as giving consent"

## On Breach Notification
- **Guidelines 9/2022 on personal data breach notification** — replaces WP250; clarifies risk thresholds, content requirements for both Art. 33 and Art. 34 notifications

## On DPIA
- **Guidelines 4/2017 on DPIA** (WP248 endorsed) — when DPIA is "likely to result in a high risk", scoring criteria, content of a valid DPIA
- National DPA "DPIA black lists" — CNIL, ICO, Garante, AEPD all publish lists of processing requiring DPIA in their jurisdiction

## On Processors
- **Guidelines 07/2020 on controllers and processors** — the controller/processor/joint-controller distinctions; Art. 28(3) DPA mandatory clauses

## On Transparency
- **Guidelines 04/2020 on transparency** — Art. 12 plain language, layered notices, machine-readable disclosure formats

## How to Cite in Reports
EDPB guidelines are persuasive, not strictly binding. The scanner can cite them as authoritative interpretation; real enforcement weight comes from a DPA decision applying the guidelines (e.g., "CNIL fined X €Y under Art. Z, applying EDPB 05/2020"). Avoid presenting an EDPB guideline as if it were itself law.
